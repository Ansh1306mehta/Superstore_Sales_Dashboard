# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard - Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Super Store Sales Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes sales, profit, quantity, delivery performance, customer segments, regions, product categories, payment modes, shipping modes, and state-level performance.

The main goal of this project is to convert sales data into meaningful business insights using interactive Power BI visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Track monthly sales and profit trends
- Analyze sales by product category
- Compare sales across different regions
- Analyze customer segments
- Understand sales by payment mode
- Compare different shipping modes
- Analyze sales and profit by state
- Track total quantity sold
- Analyze average delivery performance

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Data Visualization**
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Sales by Year

A line/area chart showing the monthly sales trend and allowing comparison between different years.

### 2. Monthly Profit by Year

A monthly profit trend that helps analyze changes in profitability over time.

### 3. Sales by Category

Sales are analyzed across the major product categories:

- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture

### 4. Sales by Payment Mode

A donut chart showing sales distribution by payment method:

- COD
- Online
- Cards

### 5. Sales by Customer Segment

Sales are analyzed across different customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 6. Sales by Region

Regional sales are compared across:

- West
- East
- Central
- South

### 7. Sales and Profit by State

A treemap is used to visualize sales and profit performance across different states.

### 8. Sales by Ship Mode

Sales are analyzed based on shipping methods:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

### 9. Sales by Sub-Category

The dashboard provides a comparison of sales across different product sub-categories.

---

## 🎛️ Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to analyze the data based on different regions and other available dimensions.

Users can select a region and interact with the visualizations to explore the corresponding sales and profit performance.

---

## 💡 Business Questions

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- What are the total sales and total profit?
- Which product category generates the highest sales?
- Which region contributes the most sales?
- Which states have high sales and profit?
- Which customer segment contributes the most sales?
- Which payment mode contributes the most sales?
- Which shipping mode generates the highest sales?
- How do sales change month by month?
- How does profit change month by month?
- Which product sub-categories generate high sales?
- What is the average delivery performance?
